Resolutions adopted by UN conferences

The following is a list of resolutions on romanization and script conversion, adopted by United Nations conferences on the standardization of geographical names. The texts of all resolutions are available on the UNGEGN website and in the resolution database. Resolutions of a general character are in bold.
